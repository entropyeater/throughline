THROUGHLINE — SYNERGY VIGNETTE LIBRARY
Non-obvious intersections that double as ad creative and onboarding examples.


INTRO PARAGRAPH (for recruiting testers)

I'm building a tool that helps people with too many interests find the one
career direction that actually unites them. You list everything you care about
and can do — work, skills, hobbies, half-formed ideas — and it maps the hidden
synergies between them to surface a direction you'd never have spotted on your
own. It takes a couple of hours and tends to reveal something genuinely
surprising. I'd love a few sharp people to run through it and tell me what
they find. Want to try it?


THE VIGNETTES

Each follows the same shape: two or three interests that seem unrelated, the
non-obvious direction their intersection points to, and why it holds. The
value is in the leap the user wouldn't have made unaided.


1. COMPETITIVE GAMING + PHYSICAL THERAPY
   Seem unrelated: one is a screen-bound hobby, one is a clinical profession.
   The intersection: esports performance and injury rehabilitation. Pro
   gamers suffer wrist, hand, and posture injuries that almost no clinician
   understands from the inside. A physical therapist who actually plays at a
   high level can build a specialist practice an outsider could never credibly
   enter.
   Why it holds: the gaming fluency is not a distraction from the career — it
   is the moat around it.


2. AMATEUR BEEKEEPING + CYBERSECURITY
   Seem unrelated: bees and breach detection.
   The intersection: distributed-systems resilience consulting, taught through
   the metaphor of the hive. A practitioner who deeply understands both can do
   unusually clear corporate training and writing on swarm behavior, failure
   tolerance, and decentralized defense. The beekeeping is the explanatory
   gift that makes the security expertise legible to executives.
   Why it holds: the obscure hobby becomes the communication edge in a field
   starved of plain language.


3. STAND-UP COMEDY + TAX ACCOUNTING
   Seem unrelated: the stage and the spreadsheet.
   The intersection: financial education content and live corporate training
   that people actually remember. The single hardest problem in accounting
   adjacency is that nobody can stay awake for it. Comedic timing is the rare
   skill that solves the exact bottleneck of the dry profession.
   Why it holds: the interests are not in different worlds — one is the
   delivery system for the other.


4. RESTORING VINTAGE MOTORCYCLES + ELDERCARE
   Seem unrelated: grease and geriatrics.
   The intersection: a workshop-based therapeutic program for older men, who
   are notoriously underserved by talk-based and craft-based care because it
   does not feel like "their" thing. Hands-on mechanical restoration is a
   proven engagement vector for exactly that population.
   Why it holds: the hobby is the trust-building medium the care profession
   cannot manufacture on its own.


5. COMPETITIVE BAKING + SUPPLY-CHAIN LOGISTICS
   Seem unrelated: croissants and container ships.
   The intersection: perishable-goods logistics consulting, or founding a
   premium short-shelf-life food brand whose entire defensibility is
   distribution. Most food entrepreneurs fail on logistics; most logistics
   experts have no feel for the product. Holding both is rare and valuable.
   Why it holds: the two interests cover each other's blind spot exactly.


6. TRANSLATION / LANGUAGE LEARNING + UX DESIGN
   Seem unrelated: literature and interfaces.
   The intersection: localization strategy and cross-cultural product design
   — not just translating strings, but designing flows that survive crossing
   a language and a culture. A designer who has felt the friction of learning
   a language from the inside designs differently.
   Why it holds: the hobby is direct, transferable domain knowledge wearing
   the disguise of a pastime.


7. LONG-DISTANCE RUNNING + WRITING SOFTWARE DOCUMENTATION
   Seem unrelated: trails and technical manuals.
   The intersection: pacing and endurance as a design philosophy for
   onboarding and developer-experience writing — work that lives or dies on
   sustaining a reader's attention across a long, effortful journey. The
   runner's intuition for managing fatigue, milestones, and second winds maps
   surprisingly directly onto structuring documentation people finish.
   Why it holds: the transferable asset is a felt sense of pacing that
   non-runners rarely articulate.


8. AQUARIUM KEEPING + REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
   Seem unrelated: fish tanks and frameworks.
   The intersection: building or consulting on monitoring systems for closed,
   sensitive systems — water chemistry and regulatory drift are the same
   problem shape: invisible parameters drifting toward a cliff, where the
   skill is noticing early and correcting calmly. The hobby trains the exact
   temperament the profession rewards.
   Why it holds: the synergy is not topical but temperamental — the same
   cognitive style serves both.


HOW TO USE THESE

As ad creative: each is a 5-7 second card — two icons that don't belong
together, the arrow, the unexpected third thing. The format itself teaches
the mechanism.

As onboarding examples: show two or three before the user builds their own
list, to prime them to enter wide and weird rather than safe and on-the-nose.
The lesson to plant: the further apart two interests seem, the more valuable
the intersection if one exists.
