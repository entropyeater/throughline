# Insight Matrix — Brand Guidelines

## Concept

A structured analytical tool that finds the single career direction sitting at the intersection of a user's scattered interests, skills, and resources. Brand position: **analytical warmth** — rigorous method in service of a deeply personal payoff.

Not a fluffy personality quiz. Not dry productivity tooling. The brand lives in the gap.

---

## Stance

**Editorial brutalist with earned warmth.** Structural mono type for data and navigation, serif display for emotional revelation moments, dark warm ink canvas throughout. The matrix grid — structure resolving from noise into illuminated clusters — is the central visual asset.

---

## Typography

| Role | Family | Usage |
|------|--------|-------|
| Display | DM Serif Display italic | Hero headlines, revelation moments, cluster names |
| Structural | JetBrains Mono | Phase labels, pair counters, scores, navigation |
| Body | DM Sans | Instructions, descriptions, notes |

- Phase labels always uppercase mono: `01 — INVENTORY`
- Counts always mono: `PAIR 35 OF 210`
- Emotional payoff always italic serif: *"Find your through-line."*

---

## Color Palette

- Background `#0D0A07` — deep warm ink
- Card `#181210` — warm dark panel
- Secondary `#241B12` — raised surface
- Foreground `#EBE3D3` — warm cream
- Muted `#7A6A58` — captions
- Primary `#C8860A` — amber gold (the illuminated intersection)
- Border `rgba(200,134,10,0.14)` — faint amber hairline

### Matrix Heat (score 1→5): `#142230` → `#1A2B22` → `#3D2C14` → `#7A4A08` → `#C8860A`

### Cluster Colors: Orange `#C8590A` · Teal `#1A8B7A` · Plum `#7A3A9A` · Indigo `#3A5A9A`

---

## Shape Language

- `--radius: 0.125rem` — nearly square, tight, anti-pill
- Borders are hairline amber: subtle, never decorative for its own sake
- The grid/matrix motif may be used as a very-low-opacity background texture

---

## Tone

- **Not:** "Find your bliss" / "Unlock your potential"
- **Yes:** "Find your through-line" / "Scatter into signal" / "The intersection you didn't see"
- Directional, earned, specific. Respects the user's analytical intelligence.
