02 — Ratings

Score the synergies.

Walk through your pairs one at a time and judge how much the two reinforce each other. This is where the hidden through-lines surface — have fun brainstorming. And don't skip the rating: it's what drives the grouping in step 3.

A few tips before you start
  • Use the arrow keys, number keys, tab, and enter to quickly add notes, select a rating, and submit it to move to the next item.
  • After this introductory window, selecting a rating will automatically move to the next cell. You can always come back to edit later.
  • Edit the question or add notes to it to customize or refine the meaning of your matrix.

About

The Insight Matrix.

The method

The Insight Matrix was developed by Charles (Chuck) Owen and Vijay Kumar, design researchers at the IIT Institute of Design in Chicago. Their structured-planning methods gave designers rigorous tools for surfacing the hidden relationships buried inside complex, messy problems.

The implementation

The interactive matrix and clustering at the heart of this tool build on Zach Pino's open implementation, published on Observable. View it on Observable →

This version

I adapted the Insight Matrix into a tool for career strategy — using it to reveal the through-lines that connect your varied interests and skills into a focused path. More of my work at DQE1.com →
